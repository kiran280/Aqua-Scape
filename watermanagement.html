Water Efficiency

Where Is Your Water Going?

Small irrigation problems can affect system performance and landscape watering.

Overwatering

Excess watering can create runoff and provide more water than required.

Poor Coverage

Incorrect sprinkler positioning can create wet and dry areas.

Evaporation

Unsuitable watering times can reduce the amount of water reaching the soil.

Leaks

Damaged pipes, valves or sprinkler components can cause water loss.
